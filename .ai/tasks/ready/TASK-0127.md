## Objective

Expose one read-only, authoritative Finding to Asset to Threat Intelligence and
Evidence to Risk Readiness projection through a dedicated API endpoint and the
existing Finding Detail UI.

The slice must compose the existing authorities. It must not create a parallel
Finding, asset, threat-intelligence, evidence, risk, decision or recommendation
engine. Missing business and environmental context remains explicit and fails
closed. `INSUFFICIENT_CONTEXT` always means no risk score, priority, business
impact, decision or recommendation.

## Existing Authorities to Reuse

- `FindingsQueryService` and the Greenbone adapter remain the Finding authority.
- `FindingAssetContextUseCase` and `AssetContextQueryService` remain the only
  Finding-to-canonical-asset and asset-criticality authority.
- `FindingThreatIntelligenceUseCase` and existing TI readers remain the only
  CVE/TI authority. Source completeness and provenance must be retained.
- `SecurityObservationCorrelationApplicationService` and canonical `Evidence`
  metadata remain the only correlation-evidence boundary.
- `RiskAssessmentReadinessService` may report whether canonical correlation
  evidence is ready for a future assessment. This is evidence readiness only.
- `RiskReadinessService` remains the only gate permitted to produce a technical
  score. It must return `INSUFFICIENT_CONTEXT` and `score = null` when any
  required risk input is `UNKNOWN` or `NOT_EVALUATED`.
- Finding Explainability remains a separate capability and must continue to
  operate unchanged.

## Projection Contract

The projection must identify its Finding and contain the following sections.

### Source Facts

- Finding ID, source, title, vendor severity and observed asset identifier.
- Each authoritative fact has a stable fact name and non-empty source reference.
- Scanner facts cite the configured Finding source without claiming business
  meaning.

### Asset Context

- Resolution status: `resolved`, `not_found` or `missing_identifier`.
- When resolved: observed identifier type/value, canonical asset ID, asset
  criticality and its exact source reference.
- Unresolved context contains no placeholder asset identity or criticality.

### Threat Intelligence and Evidence

- Preserve Finding-to-CVE applicability and each TI fact's completeness state,
  source type and source reference.
- Preserve canonical derived Evidence identifier, kind, type, contract version
  and every input reference when evidence exists.
- Preserve correlation completeness and provenance when evidence cannot be
  produced.
- TI/source failure states remain controlled API states. They must not be
  converted into absence, false, zero or a risk conclusion.

### Risk Inputs and Readiness

- Enumerate all required inputs with exactly one of `AUTHORITATIVE`, `UNKNOWN`
  or `NOT_EVALUATED`: business criticality, exposure, detection availability,
  threat-intelligence match and MITRE tactic.
- Authoritative inputs include value and source. Unknown/not-evaluated inputs
  include neither a value nor a fabricated source.
- Return the `RiskReadinessService` assessment status, available inputs, missing
  inputs and nullable score.
- Return evidence-readiness status and missing requirements separately. A
  `READY` evidence result must never imply that risk context is complete.
- When assessment status is `INSUFFICIENT_CONTEXT`, response fields are fixed:
  `score = null`, `priority = null`, `business_impact = null`,
  `decision = null`, and `recommendations = []`.
- The reason for refusal is derived from the enumerated missing input states and
  evidence requirements, not a generic invented explanation.

## DistCC Expected Fail-Closed Outcome

- Greenbone Finding facts are authoritative.
- The IP-based asset resolution may expose the configured canonical asset and
  Product-Owner-sourced `LOW` criticality.
- CVE/TI and correlation sections expose only data actually returned by their
  existing boundaries at request time.
- Exposure, detection availability, TI match and MITRE tactic are not promoted
  from Greenbone default booleans; they remain `NOT_EVALUATED` unless an
  existing authoritative boundary supplies them.
- No score, priority, impact, decision or recommendation is produced while the
  required context remains incomplete.

## Negative Guarantees

- Never call `PredatorEngine.run()`, `/api/decisions`, `DecisionEngine`, either
  legacy `RiskEngine` directly, `BusinessContextEngine`,
  `BusinessImpactBuilder`, `RecommendationEngine` or `RecommendationBuilder`.
- Do not infer business service, environment, owner, production status, data
  sensitivity, dependency, impact, risk appetite or control effectiveness.
- Do not emit fabricated score, priority, recommendation, cost, effort, risk
  reduction, confidence, target time, management conclusion or approval state.
- Do not reinterpret scanner defaults (`False`, `UNKNOWN` or `None`) as
  evaluated contextual facts.
- Do not persist the projection and do not mutate Finding, asset, TI, evidence,
  incident, hypothesis or Decision state.
- Do not invoke an AI provider. This projection is deterministic and read-only.

## API Behavior

- Exact known Finding returns a typed projection.
- Unknown Finding returns `404` without fallback data.
- Missing Finding or Asset source configuration retains existing `503`
  semantics.
- Invalid configured source data and integrity mismatches fail closed; no
  partial response may silently claim completeness.
- Existing TI timeout, unavailable, not-found and invalid-source distinctions
  are preserved through explicit controlled response/error semantics.
- Endpoint never invokes or proxies `/api/decisions`.

## Finding Detail UI

- Extend the existing selected-Finding lifecycle rather than creating a second
  Finding workspace or repository.
- Provide an explicit user action to load the risk-context projection for the
  selected Finding; expose Loading, Error and Success states.
- Reset stale risk-context state when the selected Finding changes, following
  existing explanation/TI request-version behavior.
- Render authoritative facts and source references separately from missing
  inputs and evidence requirements.
- Render an explicit refusal state for `INSUFFICIENT_CONTEXT` and visibly show
  that score, priority, business impact, decision and recommendation are not
  available.
- The UI must not calculate, summarize or infer risk/business values locally.
- Existing Finding, TI, Explainability and linked-Incident sections retain
  their behavior, routing and request boundaries.
